Live sound for your event

I run sound for my church and own a portable PA for weddings and events. I would like to take on more of that work, so if you need someone to run live audio for a ceremony, a reception, or a small event, send me an email.

On Sundays I mix on my church’s Allen & Heath SQ-5. For everything else I bring my own rig, listed below the way I write an input list.
